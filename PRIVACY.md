# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 11, 2026

Spectty is an open-source SSH terminal app for iOS. Your privacy is important to us, so we designed the app to work entirely on your device.

## Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data, usage analytics, or telemetry. Connection details and optional voice recordings are processed locally as described below.

## Network Connections

Spectty makes network connections to servers you configure and, when you choose to download the optional voice model, to Hugging Face and its file delivery services. All SSH connections are established directly between your device and the remote host. No traffic is routed through or intercepted by any intermediary server operated by us.

## Optional Voice Input

Voice input is off by default. Enabling it requires an explicit model download; the model host receives ordinary download request information, such as your IP address. Audio is transcribed entirely on your device and is never uploaded to a transcription service. Temporary recordings are deleted after transcription or cancellation. Transcripts are sent to your connected terminal only when you tap Insert Text. You can disable voice input or remove the downloaded model in Settings.

## Credentials

SSH credentials (hostnames, usernames, passwords, and keys) are stored locally on your device in the iOS Keychain. They never leave your device except as part of the SSH connection you initiate.

## Third-Party Services

Spectty does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

Spectty does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. Since Spectty collects no data, meaningful changes are unlikely.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please [open an issue](https://github.com/ocnc/spectty/issues).
